# Quantum Computing Threats to Encryption

## Key Vulnerabilities
- Shor's Algorithm can factorize large numbers exponentially faster than classical computers
- Threatens RSA, ECC (Elliptic Curve Cryptography) public-key systems
- Symmetric encryption less impacted, but key sizes must increase

## Encryption Methods at Risk
1. RSA (most vulnerable)
2. Diffie-Hellman key exchange
3. Elliptic Curve Cryptography

## Estimated Timeline
- Near-term threat (10-15 years): Partial quantum capabilities
- Long-term threat (20-30 years): Full cryptographic break potential

## Recommended Actions
- Transition to quantum-resistant algorithms
- Implement hybrid classical-quantum encryption
- Continuous monitoring of quantum computing advancements
