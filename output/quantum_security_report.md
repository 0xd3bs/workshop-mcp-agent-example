# Quantum Computing Encryption Threats: Technical Verification Report

## Encryption Vulnerability Assessment

### Threat Landscape
- **Critical Finding**: Current public-key cryptosystems are fundamentally vulnerable to large-scale quantum computers
- Potential complete compromise of digital communication confidentiality and integrity

### Technical Timeline Projection
- Estimated Quantum Threat Window: Within 20 years
- Infrastructure Transition Period: Approximately 15-20 years
- Urgency: Immediate preparatory actions required

## Mitigation Framework

### Quantum-Resistant Cryptography Standards
NIST has developed critical standards to address quantum computing threats:

1. **FIPS 203**: Module-Lattice-Based Key-Encapsulation Mechanism
2. **FIPS 204**: Module-Lattice-Based Digital Signature Standard
3. **FIPS 205**: Stateless Hash-Based Digital Signature Standard

### Key Technical Considerations
- Develop cryptographic systems secure against quantum and classical computing
- Maintain protocol interoperability
- Proactive, anticipatory approach to cryptographic evolution

## Recommended Actions
1. Assess current cryptographic infrastructure
2. Begin quantum-resistant algorithm integration
3. Continuously monitor NIST post-quantum cryptography developments
