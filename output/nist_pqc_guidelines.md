# NIST Post-Quantum Cryptography Guidelines

## Key Developments (as of 2024-2025)

### Standardized Algorithms
1. **FIPS 203**: Module-Lattice-Based Key-Encapsulation Mechanism Standard
2. **FIPS 204**: Module-Lattice-Based Digital Signature Standard
3. **FIPS 205**: Stateless Hash-Based Digital Signature Standard

### Critical Guidelines

#### Quantum Threat Assessment
- Large-scale quantum computers could break current public-key cryptosystems
- Potential serious compromise of digital communication confidentiality
- Estimated quantum threat window: Within 20 years

#### Cryptographic Transition Recommendations
1. Begin immediate preparation for quantum-resistant infrastructure
2. Develop systems secure against both quantum and classical computers
3. Ensure interoperability with existing communication protocols
4. Implement incremental cryptographic upgrades

### Standardization Process Highlights
- Initiated quantum-resistant algorithm selection process in 2016
- Continuous evaluation of potential post-quantum cryptographic solutions
- Focus on developing quantum-resistant public-key cryptographic algorithms

### Implementation Guidance
- Prioritize lattice-based and hash-based cryptographic mechanisms
- Develop hybrid classical-quantum encryption strategies
- Continuously monitor quantum computing technological advancements

**Note**: Guidelines are subject to updates as quantum computing technology evolves.
