# Lattice-Based Cryptography for Quantum Resistance

## Overview
Lattice-based cryptography represents a leading approach to developing quantum-resistant cryptographic systems.

## Key Characteristics
- Mathematical foundation: Complex geometric structures called lattices
- Computational hardness: Based on solving difficult mathematical problems
- Resistance mechanism: Challenges quantum algorithms in solving certain mathematical problems

## Major Algorithmic Approaches
1. Learning With Errors (LWE)
2. Ring Learning With Errors (R-LWE)
3. Module Learning With Errors (M-LWE)

## Security Advantages
- Believed to be resistant to Shor's and Grover's quantum algorithms
- Compact key sizes compared to other post-quantum alternatives
- Flexible for various cryptographic primitives

## Current NIST Standardization Status
- Crystals-Kyber selected as primary key encapsulation mechanism
- Strong candidate in Post-Quantum Cryptography standardization process

## Potential Limitations
- Larger key and signature sizes
- Computational overhead
- Ongoing research for optimization
